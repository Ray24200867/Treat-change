TREATS Inclusive Scholarship Scheme

TREATS Inclusive Scholarship Scheme aims to recognize and support local youth with all abilities and background to pursue their aspirations in different aspects. By unleashing awardees’ potential and talent through learning and development opportunities, they are empowered for their full participation in society.

Up to 10 awardees for each year. Each awardee will be granted with a scholarship of a maximum value of HK$10,0000 per year. The course / training fees, necessary equipment, meals, travel and transportation expenses incurred by the course / training will be covered by the scholarship amount.

Target:

  • Youth with special educational needs and/or from ethnic minorities; AND
  • Participated in previous TREATS‘ programmes/activities who has demonstrated enthusiasm, potential and a positive learning attitude; AND
  • Interested in further their development and learning in sports, arts or design, information technology or other extra-curricular activities

Kwan Yat Sing

Yat Sing is a young man passionate about coffee art, undeterred by the challenges of mild intellectual disability. His journey began with a fascination for latte art, leading him to learn everything from brewing to creating intricate designs. Despite difficulties like shaky hands while frothing milk, he practiced diligently at a coffee shop, refining his skills. Yat Sing believes everyone has unique brilliance and aims to share warmth and passion through his coffee creations. His story exemplifies how enthusiasm and perseverance can help anyone brew their own special “coffee” in life, inspiring others to pursue their dreams.

Read his story (Chinese only)

Leung Joshua

Joshua, a recent high school graduate with autism and speech difficulties, found confidence and expression through music. Starting piano at six, he has participated in many competitions, even representing Hong Kong in the World Championships of Performing Arts in the U.S. In his Form5, he composed “Omicron” in a pop music course to inspire others facing challenges. Joshua performs in care homes for the elderly and disabled, using music to connect with society and spread warmth. He believes in music’s healing power and aims to raise awareness for vulnerable communities, creating a symphony of love and support.
